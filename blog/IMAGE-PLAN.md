## Screenshot privacy checklist

Never publish a screenshot until it has been checked for:

- terminal prompts, usernames, home paths, command history, or model paths;
- IP addresses, MAC addresses, ports, hostnames, QR codes, or device IDs;
- browser address bars, bookmarks, account avatars, notifications, or open tabs;
- management consoles, relay controls, network maps, or credentials;
- EXIF, GPS, camera serial, or editing-history metadata.

Prefer cropped application-only captures. Store public exports separately from raw screenshots.
